## 🎬 My Favorite Movie: *Love You To The Stars and Back* (2017)

**Directed By:** Antoinette Jadaone
**Genre:** Romance / Drama / Adventure / Comedy

### Overview

A girl named Mika decides to go on a road trip to look for aliens. During her trip, she accidentally bumps into Caloy, whom she gives a free ride to until he reaches his destination. Both embark on a journey filled with adventures and misadventures, not knowing they would fall in love with each other along the way

## 🌟 Key Themes

The film beautifully balances comedy with heavy emotional realities.

> "Sometimes, two broken people met together not to fix each other, but to share the journey."

- **Grief and Acceptance:** Processing the loss of a parent.
- **Resilience:** Maintaining optimism in the face of terminal illness.
- **Accidental Connections:** Finding comfort in unexpected companionship.

## 📝 My Personal Review & Rating

**Rating:** ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ (5/5)

**Thoughts:** This movie is a roller-coaster of emotions. It starts as a quirky, lighthearted alien-hunting road trip but smoothly transitions into a deeply moving story about life, love, and coping with mortality. Life isn't merely about finding what is lost, but about realizing that even the most unexpected paths can give us direction.
